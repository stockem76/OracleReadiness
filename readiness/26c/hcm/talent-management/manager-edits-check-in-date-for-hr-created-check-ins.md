# Manager Edits Check-in Date for HR Created Check-Ins

`⚙️ Steps to Enable`

> 📖 [View on Oracle Help Center](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/readiness/hcm/26c/tama-26c/26C-talent-mgmt-wn-f50596.htm)

When the HR Specialist creates check-ins either by using the **Create Check-Ins** menu button on the Mass Actions Processes for Check-In Documents page or **Create Check-In** button in the Check-In Documents page, the HR Specialist can enter any check-in date.

HR Creates Check-Ins

Managers can now edit the check-in date in a check-in scheduled by an HR Specialist.

Manager Edits Check-Ins

## 🎯 Business Benefit

• As a manager, you can modify the check-in date.

## ⚙️ Steps to Enable and Configure

You don't need to do anything to enable this feature.
